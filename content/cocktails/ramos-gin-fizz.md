slug: ramos-gin-fizz
tags:
  - gin
  - egg white
  - cream
  - orange flower water
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 6 cl (2 oz) gin
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) lemon juice
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) lime juice
  - 3 cl (1 oz) simple syrup
  - 6 cl (2 oz) cream
  - 1 egg white
  - 2 dashes orange flower water
  - Soda water to top
steps:
  - Dry shake all but soda to emulsify.
  - Shake with ice until very frothy and chilled.
  - Strain into a tall glass and top with soda.
