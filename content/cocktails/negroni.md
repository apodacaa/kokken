layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 3 cl (1 oz) gin
  - 3 cl (1 oz) Campari
  - 3 cl (1 oz) sweet vermouth
steps:
  - Stir with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a rocks glass over ice.
  - Garnish with orange peel.
