tags:

  - maraschino
  - violette
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 4.5 cl (1 1/2 oz) gin
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) maraschino liqueur
  - 0.75 cl (1/4 oz) crème de violette
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) lemon juice
steps:
  - Shake with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with cherry.
