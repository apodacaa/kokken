  - maraschino
  - violette
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk

ingredients:
  - 1 1/2 oz (4.5 cl) gin
  - 1/2 oz (1.5 cl) maraschino liqueur
  - 1/4 oz (0.75 cl) crème de violette
  - 1/2 oz (1.5 cl) lemon juice
steps:
  - Shake with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with cherry.
