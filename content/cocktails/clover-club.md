---
title: Clover Club
slug: clover-club
tags:
  - gin
  - raspberry
  - egg white
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 6 cl (2 oz) gin
  - 2 cl (2/3 oz) lemon juice
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) raspberry syrup
  - 1 egg white
steps:
  - Dry shake all ingredients.
  - Add ice and shake again until well chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with raspberries.
---
