---
title: Clover Club
slug: clover-club
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 2 oz (6 cl) gin
  - 2/3 oz (2 cl) lemon juice
  - 1/2 oz (1.5 cl) raspberry syrup
  - 1 egg white
steps:
  - Dry shake all ingredients.
  - Add ice and shake again until well chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with raspberries.
---
