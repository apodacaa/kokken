---
title: Bee’s Knees
slug: bees-knees
tags:
  - gin
  - honey
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 2 oz (6 cl) gin
  - 2/3 oz (2 cl) lemon juice
  - 2/3 oz (2 cl) honey syrup
steps:
  - Shake with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with lemon twist.
---
