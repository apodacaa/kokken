title: Sazerac
slug: sazerac
tags:
  - whiskey
  - absinthe
  - bitters
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 2 oz (6 cl) rye whiskey
  - 1 sugar cube
  - 3 dashes Peychaud’s bitters
  - Absinthe rinse
steps:
  - Muddle sugar and bitters; add whiskey and ice, stir.
  - Rinse chilled glass with absinthe.
  - Strain into the rinsed glass.
