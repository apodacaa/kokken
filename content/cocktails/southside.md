---
title: Southside
slug: southside
tags:
  - gin
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 2 oz London dry gin
  - 3/4 oz lime juice
  - 3/4 oz Simple Syrup
  - 8 fresh mint leaves
  - 1 mint sprig for garnish
steps:
  - Add all ingredients except sprig to shaker.
  - Shake for 15 seconds.
  - Strain and add mint sprig for garnish.
