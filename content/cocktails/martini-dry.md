---
title: Martini (Dry)
slug: martini-dry
tags:
  - gin
  - vermouth
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 7.5 cl (2 1/2 oz) gin
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) dry vermouth
steps:
  - Stir with ice until very cold.
  - Strain into a chilled coupe.
  - Garnish with olive or lemon twist.
---
