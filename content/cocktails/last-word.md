title: Last Word
slug: last-word
tags:
  - gin
  - chartreuse
  - maraschino
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 2.25 cl (3/4 oz) gin
  - 2.25 cl (3/4 oz) green Chartreuse
  - 2.25 cl (3/4 oz) maraschino liqueur
  - 2.25 cl (3/4 oz) lime juice
steps:
  - Shake with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with cherry.
