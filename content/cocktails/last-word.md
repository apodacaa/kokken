---
title: Last Word
slug: last-word
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 3/4 oz (2.25 cl) gin
  - 3/4 oz (2.25 cl) green Chartreuse
  - 3/4 oz (2.25 cl) maraschino liqueur
  - 3/4 oz (2.25 cl) lime juice
steps:
  - Shake with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with cherry.
---
