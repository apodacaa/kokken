---
title: French 75
slug: french-75
tags:
  - gin
  - champagne
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 3 cl (1 oz) gin
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) lemon juice
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) simple syrup
  - 9 cl (3 oz) Champagne
steps:
  - Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice; strain into a flute.
  - Top with Champagne.
---
