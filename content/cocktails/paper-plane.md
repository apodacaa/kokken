---
title: Paper Plane
slug: paper-plane
tags:
  - whiskey
  - aperol
  - amaro
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 3 cl (1 oz) bourbon
  - 3 cl (1 oz) Aperol
  - 3 cl (1 oz) Amaro Nonino
  - 3 cl (1 oz) lemon juice
steps:
  - Shake with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with lemon twist.
