---
title: Tom Collins
slug: tom-collins
tags:
  - gin
  - soda water
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 6 cl (2 oz) gin
  - 3 cl (1 oz) lemon juice
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) simple syrup
  - Soda water to top
steps:
  - Shake first three with ice and strain into a glass with ice.
  - Top with soda water.
  - Garnish with lemon slice.
---
