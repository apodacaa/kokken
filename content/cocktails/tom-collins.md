---
title: Tom Collins
slug: tom-collins
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 2 oz (6 cl) gin
  - 1 oz (3 cl) lemon juice
  - 1/2 oz (1.5 cl) simple syrup
  - Soda water to top
steps:
  - Shake first three with ice and strain into a glass with ice.
  - Top with soda water.
  - Garnish with lemon slice.
---
