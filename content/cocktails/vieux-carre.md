ingredients:
  - 3 cl (1 oz) rye whiskey
  - 3 cl (1 oz) cognac
  - 3 cl (1 oz) sweet vermouth
  - 1 barspoon Bénédictine
  - 2 dashes Peychaud’s bitters
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters
steps:
  - Stir with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a rocks glass.
  - Garnish with lemon twist.
