ingredients:
  - 1 oz London dry gin
  - 1 oz Lillet Blanc
  - 3/4 oz orange liqueur
  - 3/4 oz lemon juice
  - 1/4 oz Simple Syrup
  - 1/8 teaspoon absinthe
  - Orange twist
steps:
  - Add everything but orange twist in shaker.
  - Fill shaker with ice.
  - Shake for 15 seconds.
  - Strain into glass and add orange twist.
