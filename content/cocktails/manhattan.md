ingredients:
  - 2 oz (6 cl) rye whiskey
  - 1 oz (3 cl) sweet vermouth
  - 2 dashes Angostura bitters
steps:
  - Stir with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with cherry.
