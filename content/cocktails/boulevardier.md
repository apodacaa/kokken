---
title: Boulevardier
slug: boulevardier
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 1 oz (3 cl) bourbon
  - 1 oz (3 cl) Campari
  - 1 oz (3 cl) sweet vermouth
steps:
  - Stir with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe or rocks glass.
  - Garnish with orange peel.
---
