---
title: Whiskey Sour
slug: whiskey-sour
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 2 oz (6 cl) bourbon
  - 3/4 oz (2.5 cl) lemon juice
  - 3/4 oz (2.5 cl) simple syrup
  - Optional: 1 egg white
steps:
  - Shake all ingredients with ice.
  - Strain into a rocks or coupe.
  - Garnish with cherry or lemon.
---
