---
title: Gimlet
slug: gimlet
tags:
  - gin
layout: layouts/cocktail.njk
permalink: /cocktails/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - 6 cl (2 oz) gin
  - 2 cl (2/3 oz) lime juice
  - 1.5 cl (1/2 oz) simple syrup
steps:
  - Shake with ice until chilled.
  - Strain into a coupe.
  - Garnish with lime wheel.
---
