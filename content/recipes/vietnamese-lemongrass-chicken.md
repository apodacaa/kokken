title: Vietnamese Lemongrass Chicken
slug: vietnamese-lemongrass-chicken
tags:
  - main

layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Riesling (off-dry) – acidity balances lemongrass
    - Grüner Veltliner – peppery freshness
  beer:
    - Pilsner – crisp
    - Saison – herbal, dry
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 lb (500 g) chicken thigh, sliced
      - 2 stalks lemongrass, minced (tender part)
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) fish sauce
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) soy sauce
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) sugar
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil
      - Scallions, lime
steps:
  - Marinate chicken with lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, soy, and sugar (20–30 minutes).
  - Stir-fry in hot oil until caramelized and cooked through.
  - Top with scallions; serve with lime over rice.
