title: Lentil Soup with Carrots & Celery
slug: lentil-soup-carrots-celery
tags:
  - soup
  - vegetarian
  - vegan
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 2 tbsp olive oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 carrots, diced
      - 2 celery stalks, diced
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 250 g (1 1/4 cups) brown or green lentils, rinsed
      - 1.2 L (5 cups) vegetable stock
      - 1 tsp cumin
      - Salt, pepper, lemon
steps:
  - Sauté onion, carrots, and celery in oil; add garlic.
  - Add lentils, stock, and cumin; simmer 30–35 minutes until tender.
  - Season; finish with lemon.
