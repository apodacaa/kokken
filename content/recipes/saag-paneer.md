pairings:
  wine:
    - Gewürztraminer – aromatic
    - Riesling – off-dry
  beer:
    - Pale Ale – bitterness cuts cream
    - Hefeweizen – fruity
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 14 oz (400 g) paneer, cubed
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) oil or ghee
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) grated ginger
      - 1 green chili, chopped
      - 1 lb (500 g) spinach (fresh or frozen)
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) garam masala
      - 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) cumin
      - 2/3 cup (150 ml) cream or yogurt
      - Salt
steps:
  - Brown paneer in 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil; set aside.
  - Sauté onion, garlic, ginger, and chili; add spinach and spices; cook until wilted.
  - Blend partly if desired; stir in cream and paneer; simmer 5 minutes. Season.
