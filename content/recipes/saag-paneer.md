---
title: Saag Paneer
slug: saag-paneer
tags:
  - main
  - vegetarian
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 400 g (14 oz) paneer, cubed
      - 2 tbsp oil or ghee
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tbsp grated ginger
      - 1 green chili, chopped
      - 500 g (1 lb) spinach (fresh or frozen)
      - 1 tsp garam masala
      - 1/2 tsp cumin
      - 150 ml (2/3 cup) cream or yogurt
      - Salt
steps:
  - Brown paneer in 1 tbsp oil; set aside.
  - Sauté onion, garlic, ginger, and chili; add spinach and spices; cook until wilted.
  - Blend partly if desired; stir in cream and paneer; simmer 5 minutes. Season.
