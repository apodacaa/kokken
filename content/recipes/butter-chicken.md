---
title: Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
slug: butter-chicken
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Off-dry Riesling – a little sweetness soothes the spice and cuts the cream
    - Viognier – floral and soft, matches the tomato-butter richness
  beer:
    - Hefeweizen – soft, low bitterness, banana and clove play with garam masala
    - Amber Lager – malt sweetness rounds off the sauce
ingredients:
  - section: Marinade
    items:
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs
      - 1/3 cup (80 g) plain yogurt
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) vegetable oil
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) ginger-garlic paste
      - 1 tsp garam masala
      - 1 tsp salt
      - 1/4 tsp saffron threads, crushed (or a pinch of turmeric)
  - section: Sauce
    items:
      - 3 tbsp (45 g) cultured unsalted butter
      - 6 green cardamom pods
      - 1 black cardamom pod
      - 1 cinnamon stick
      - 4 cloves
      - 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
      - 2 fresh green chiles (serrano), split
      - 3 tbsp (45 ml) ginger-garlic paste (about 1 1/2 inch ginger and 6 cloves garlic, pureed)
      - 28 oz (800 g) canned whole tomatoes with juices
      - 2 tbsp (30 g) tomato paste
      - 1 tsp salt, to taste
  - section: Finish
    items:
      - 1 tbsp (15 g) cold butter
      - 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed
      - 1 tsp garam masala
      - 1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream, plus more for drizzling
      - Cilantro leaves
steps:
  - Blend the ginger and garlic to a paste. Mix 2 tsp into the yogurt with the oil, garam masala, salt, and saffron. Coat the chicken and refrigerate at least 4 hr, ideally overnight.
  - Blend the tomatoes with their juices and the tomato paste until smooth.
  - Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Fry the cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves until the cloves puff up. Add the fenugreek seeds, chiles, and the 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste; sauté until browned and fragrant.
  - Add the tomato purée. Simmer over medium heat, stirring often, 40–45 min, until reduced to a thick, paste-like sauce. This slow reduction caramelizes the tomatoes, so no sugar is needed.
  - Meanwhile, set an oven rack about 4 inches below the broiler and heat it on high. Broil the chicken on a foil-lined rack 10–14 min, flipping once, until charred in spots but still slightly underdone inside. Rest 10 min, then cut into large chunks.
  - Off the heat, pass the sauce through a sieve into a clean pot, pressing on the solids to discard the whole spices.
  - Add the chicken and any juices, the salt, the kasuri methi (crushed between your palms), and the garam masala. Simmer gently 6–8 min until the chicken is cooked through.
  - Off the heat, stir in the cream and the cold butter until glossy; do not boil after this or the sauce can split. Season, drizzle with cream, and top with cilantro.
notes: No cashews, so the body comes from the long tomato reduction, the straining, and the cream and finishing butter. Pull the chicken slightly underdone because it finishes in the sauce, and a fully cooked piece turns dry. Cultured butter has more butter flavor than regular; ghee works but tastes less rich. For a quicker weeknight version, skip the whole spices and use 1 tbsp garam masala, 1/2 cup tomato paste and 1 1/2 cups water instead of canned tomatoes, and marinate only 30 min.
