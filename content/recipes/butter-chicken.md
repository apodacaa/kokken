---
title: Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)
slug: butter-chicken
tags:
  - main
  - chicken
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 700 g (1 1/2 lb) boneless chicken thighs, chunks
      - 150 g (2/3 cup) plain yogurt
      - 2 tbsp lemon juice
      - 1 tbsp garam masala
      - 2 tsp ground cumin
      - 2 tsp paprika
      - 3 tbsp butter
      - 1 onion, finely chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tbsp grated ginger
      - 400 g (14 oz) canned tomatoes
      - 250 ml (1 cup) cream
      - Salt, cilantro
steps:
  - Marinate chicken in yogurt, lemon, garam masala, cumin, and paprika for at least 1 hour.
  - Brown chicken; remove. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger in butter until golden.
  - Add tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes then blend smooth. Return chicken; stir in cream; simmer 10 minutes. Season and garnish.
