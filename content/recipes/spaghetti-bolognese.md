ingredients:
  - section: Sauce
    items:
      - 500 g (1 lb) ground beef
      - 1 onion, diced
      - 1 carrot, diced
      - 1 celery stalk, diced
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 700 ml (24 oz) tomato passata
      - 100 ml (1/2 cup) milk
      - 2 tbsp olive oil
      - Salt, pepper, Parmesan
  - section: Pasta
    items:
      - 500 g (1 lb) spaghetti
steps:
  - Sauté onion, carrot, and celery in oil; add garlic. Add beef; brown well.
  - Stir in passata and milk; simmer 30–45 minutes; season.
  - Cook spaghetti; toss with sauce; serve with Parmesan.
