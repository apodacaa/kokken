---
title: Lamb Kofta with Yogurt Sauce
slug: lamb-kofta-with-yogurt-sauce
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Syrah – smoky, savory
    - Grenache – fruity, spicy red
  beer:
    - Brown Ale – nutty
    - Belgian Dubbel – dried fruit complexity
ingredients:
  - section: Kofta
    items:
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) ground lamb
      - 1 small onion, grated
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) ground coriander
      - 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) cinnamon
      - Salt and pepper
  - section: Yogurt Sauce
    items:
      - 3/4 cup (200 g) Greek yogurt
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) lemon juice
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) mint, chopped
steps:
  - Mix lamb with onion, garlic, spices, salt, and pepper; form logs or patties.
  - Grill or pan-sear 3–4 minutes per side until 160°F (71°C).
  - Combine yogurt, lemon, and mint; serve alongside.
