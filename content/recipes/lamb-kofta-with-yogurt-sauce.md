ingredients:
  - section: Kofta
    items:
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) ground lamb
      - 1 small onion, grated
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tsp ground cumin
      - 1 tsp ground coriander
      - 1/2 tsp cinnamon
      - Salt and pepper
  - section: Yogurt Sauce
    items:
      - 3/4 cup (200 g) Greek yogurt
      - 1 tbsp lemon juice
      - 1 tbsp mint, chopped
steps:
  - Mix lamb with onion, garlic, spices, salt, and pepper; form logs or patties.
  - Grill or pan-sear 3–4 minutes per side until 70°C / 160°F.
  - Combine yogurt, lemon, and mint; serve alongside.
