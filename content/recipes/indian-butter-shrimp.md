ingredients:
  - section: Marinade
    items:
      - 1/4 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
      - 2 tsp ground cumin
      - 2 tsp paprika (or hot paprika for more heat, or mix 1 1/2 tsp paprika + 1/2 tsp chili powder for smoky spice)
      - 2 tsp garam masala
      - 2 tsp fresh lime juice
      - 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
      - 1 tsp grated ginger
      - 1 garlic clove, grated or minced
      - 2 lbs large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  - section: Sauce
    items:
      - 4 tbsp (1/2 stick) unsalted butter
      - 2 shallots, minced
      - 2 garlic cloves, grated or minced
      - 1 1/2 tsp grated ginger
      - 1/4 to 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes, to taste
      - 1/4 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
      - 1 (28-oz) can diced tomatoes with juices
      - 1 cup heavy cream
      - 1/2 tsp grated lime zest
      - Cooked basmati rice, for serving
      - Chopped fresh cilantro, for serving
steps:
  - Combine yogurt, cumin, paprika, garam masala, lime juice, salt, ginger, and garlic. Stir in shrimp. Cover and refrigerate 15 to 60 minutes.
  - Melt 2 tbsp butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add shallots and a pinch of salt; cook until golden, 5-7 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, red pepper flakes, and 1/4 tsp salt; cook 1-2 minutes.
  - Stir in tomatoes with their juices. Simmer uncovered over medium-low, stirring occasionally, until thickened, 20-25 minutes.
  - Add cream and simmer 5-7 minutes more until sauce is silky. Season with salt.
  - Add marinated shrimp with marinade, remaining 2 tbsp butter, and lime zest. Cook over medium heat until shrimp are pink, 3-5 minutes.
  - Serve over basmati rice and top with cilantro.
