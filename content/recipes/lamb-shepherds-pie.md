---
title: Lamb Shepherd’s Pie
slug: lamb-shepherds-pie
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Rioja Reserva – savory oak and leather, medium tannin, a classic with lamb
    - Côtes-du-Rhône – grenache/syrah warmth and black pepper against the thyme
  beer:
    - ESB – the pub classic; malt sweetness meets the carrots and peas
    - Stout – roasted depth, best if you swap the wine for a splash of it
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 3/4 lb (800 g) ground lamb
      - 1 onion, diced
      - 2 carrots, diced
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) tomato paste
      - 1/2 cup (120 ml) dry red wine
      - 1 cup (250 ml) beef stock
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) Worcestershire sauce
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) thyme
      - 1 bay leaf
      - 1 cup (150 g) frozen peas
      - 2 lb (900 g) potatoes, peeled
      - 1/2 cup (100 ml) milk
      - 4 tbsp (50 g) butter
steps:
  - Boil potatoes in salted water until tender; drain and mash with butter and milk.
  - Heat a little oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over high heat. Add the lamb and brown well, breaking it up as it cooks. Spoon off excess fat, leaving a few tablespoons behind.
  - Add onion and carrot to the rendered fat and cook until softened; add garlic and cook 1 minute.
  - Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 minute. Add red wine and simmer until nearly evaporated, scraping up the browned bits.
  - Add stock, thyme, bay leaf, and Worcestershire; simmer 20 minutes until thick. Discard the bay leaf and stir in the peas.
  - Spread meat mixture in a baking dish and top evenly with mashed potatoes.
  - Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 25–30 minutes until the top is golden.
  - Rest 15–20 minutes before serving so the filling sets.
