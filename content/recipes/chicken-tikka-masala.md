ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) chicken thighs, chunks
      - 2/3 cup (150 g) yogurt
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) grated ginger
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) garam masala
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) paprika
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) butter
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 14 oz (400 g) canned tomatoes
      - 2/3 cup (150 ml) cream
      - Salt, cilantro
steps:
  - Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger, garlic, and spices (1 hour+); broil or pan-sear until charred.
  - Sauté onion in butter; add tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes; blend smooth.
  - Add chicken and cream; simmer 5–10 minutes; season and garnish.
