ingredients:
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 1 tbsp oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 2 cans (800 g / 28 oz) black beans, drained
      - 1 tsp cumin
  - section: Crema
    items:
      - 1 avocado
      - 60 ml (1/4 cup) yogurt
      - 1 tbsp lime juice
      - Salt
  - section: To Serve
    items:
      - Corn tortillas
      - Toppings: cabbage, salsa, cilantro
steps:
  - Sauté onion and garlic; add beans and cumin; mash slightly; season.
  - Blend avocado, yogurt, lime, and salt.
  - Warm tortillas; fill with beans; top with crema and toppings.
