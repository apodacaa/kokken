---
title: Black Bean Tacos with Avocado Crema
slug: black-bean-tacos-avocado-crema
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Albariño – lime and salinity brighten avocado and beans
    - Dry Rosé of Grenache – berry fruit and acidity for the cabbage and salsa
  beer:
    - Mexican Lager – clean and cold against creamy avocado
    - Vienna Lager – toasty malt for the cumin-spiced beans
ingredients:
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 2 cans (800 g / 28 oz) black beans, drained
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) cumin
  - section: Crema
    items:
      - 1 avocado
      - 1/4 cup (60 ml) yogurt
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) lime juice
      - Salt
  - section: Toppings
    items:
      - Cabbage
      - Salsa
      - Cilantro
steps:
  - Sauté onion and garlic; add beans and cumin; mash slightly; season.
  - Blend avocado, yogurt, lime, and salt.
  - Warm tortillas; fill with beans; top with crema and toppings.
