---
title: Creamy Potato Soup with Leeks
slug: creamy-potato-soup-with-leeks
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Muscadet sur Lie – mineral and saline against creamy potato
    - Chablis – steely acidity for the leeks and cream
  beer:
    - Kölsch – delicate and clean
    - Helles – soft malt lets the leek flavor through
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) butter
      - 2 leeks, sliced
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 lb (900 g) potatoes, diced
      - 1 L (4 cups) stock
      - 1 cup (250 ml) cream or milk
steps:
  - Sauté leeks and onion in butter until soft.
  - Add potatoes and stock; simmer 20 minutes until tender.
  - Blend until smooth; stir in cream, season to taste.
---
