---
title: Keema Matar (Spiced Ground Lamb with Peas)
slug: keema-matar
tags:
  - main
  - lamb
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 700 g (1 1/2 lb) ground lamb
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic & 1 tbsp ginger, minced
      - 2 tsp garam masala
      - 1 tsp ground cumin
      - 1 tsp paprika
      - 400 g (14 oz) canned tomatoes
      - 150 g (1 cup) peas
      - Salt, cilantro
steps:
  - Brown lamb; drain excess fat. Add onion, garlic, and ginger; sauté 3–4 minutes.
  - Add spices; cook 1 minute. Add tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes.
  - Stir in peas; heat through. Season and garnish.
