---
title: Keema Matar (Spiced Ground Lamb with Peas)
slug: keema-matar
tags:
  - main
  - lamb
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Shiraz – peppery, bold
    - Tempranillo – earthy & rustic
  beer:
    - Amber Ale – balances spice
    - Brown Ale – nutty with lamb
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) ground lamb
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic & 1 tbsp (15 ml) ginger, minced
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) garam masala
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) paprika
      - 14 oz (400 g) canned tomatoes
      - 1 cup (150 g) peas
      - Salt, cilantro
steps:
  - Brown lamb; drain excess fat. Add onion, garlic, and ginger; sauté 3–4 minutes.
  - Add spices; cook 1 minute. Add tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes.
  - Stir in peas; heat through. Season and garnish.
