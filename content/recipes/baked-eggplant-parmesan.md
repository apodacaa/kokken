---
title: Baked Eggplant Parmesan
slug: baked-eggplant-parmesan
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Barbera d’Asti – high acid and soft tannin match the tomato without fighting the cheese
    - Chianti Classico – savory cherry and acidity cut through baked mozzarella
  beer:
    - Vienna Lager – toasty malt echoes the breadcrumb crust
    - Dunkel – soft roasted malt suits caramelized eggplant
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 2 eggplants, sliced
      - 2 cups breadcrumbs
      - 2 eggs, beaten
      - 24 oz (700 ml) marinara
      - 2 cups (200 g) mozzarella, shredded
      - 1/2 cup (50 g) Parmesan, grated
steps:
  - Dip eggplant slices in beaten egg, then breadcrumbs; bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20 minutes until golden.
  - Layer marinara, baked eggplant, mozzarella, and Parmesan in a baking dish.
  - Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 30 minutes until bubbly.
