---
title: Malaysian Laksa Noodle Soup
slug: malaysian-laksa-noodle-soup
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Off-dry Riesling – sweetness and acidity for the chili and coconut
    - Gewürztraminer – lychee and spice suit the laksa paste
  beer:
    - Witbier – citrus and coriander with shrimp and lime
    - Lager – crisp relief; avoid heavy hops with the heat
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil
      - 2–3 tbsp (30–45 ml) laksa paste
      - 14 oz (400 ml) coconut milk
      - 2 cups (500 ml) chicken or veg stock
      - 7 oz (200 g) shrimp or chicken
      - 7 oz (200 g) rice noodles
      - Bean sprouts, lime, cilantro
steps:
  - Fry laksa paste in oil; add coconut milk and stock; simmer 10 minutes.
  - Add shrimp/chicken; cook through. Add cooked noodles.
  - Serve topped with bean sprouts, lime, and cilantro.
---
