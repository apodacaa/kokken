title: Malaysian Laksa Noodle Soup
slug: malaysian-laksa-noodle-soup
tags:
  - soup
  - noodles
  - seafood
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 tbsp oil
      - 2–3 tbsp laksa paste
      - 400 ml (14 oz) coconut milk
      - 500 ml (2 cups) chicken or veg stock
      - 200 g (7 oz) shrimp or chicken
      - 200 g (7 oz) rice noodles
      - Bean sprouts, lime, cilantro
steps:
  - Fry laksa paste in oil; add coconut milk and stock; simmer 10 minutes.
  - Add shrimp/chicken; cook through. Add cooked noodles.
  - Serve topped with bean sprouts, lime, and cilantro.
