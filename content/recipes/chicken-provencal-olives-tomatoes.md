title: Chicken Provencal with Olives & Tomatoes
slug: chicken-provencal-olives-tomatoes
tags:
  - main
  - chicken
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 4 chicken thighs
      - 1 onion, sliced
      - 400 g (14 oz) tomatoes (canned or fresh)
      - 100 g (1/2 cup) olives
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
steps:
  - Brown chicken in a little oil; remove and set aside.
  - Sauté onion and garlic; add tomatoes and olives.
  - Return chicken to pan and simmer 30 minutes until tender.
