---
title: Chicken Provencal with Olives & Tomatoes
slug: chicken-provencal-olives-tomatoes
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Côtes du Rhône – peppery Grenache/Syrah
    - Provence Rosé – dry & herbal
  beer:
    - Biere de Garde – rustic French style
    - Belgian Blonde – light spice & fruit
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 4 chicken thighs
      - 1 onion, sliced
      - 14 oz (400 g) tomatoes (canned or fresh)
      - 1/2 cup (100 g) olives
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
steps:
  - Brown chicken in a little oil; remove and set aside.
  - Sauté onion and garlic; add tomatoes and olives.
  - Return chicken to pan and simmer 30 minutes until tender.
