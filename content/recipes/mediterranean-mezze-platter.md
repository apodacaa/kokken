  wine:
    - Assyrtiko – briny and bright against olives, feta and lemon

    - Pilsner – crisp against olives and creamy dips

ingredients:
  - section: Components
    items:
      - Hummus, tzatziki, olives, feta
      - Falafel or roasted chickpeas
      - Pita bread, cucumbers, tomatoes, herbs
      - Olive oil, lemon
steps:
  - Arrange dips, cheeses, and olives on a platter.
  - Add warm pita and fresh veg; drizzle with olive oil and lemon.
