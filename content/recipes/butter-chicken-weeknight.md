pairings:
  wine:
    - Off-dry Riesling – a little sweetness soothes the spice and cuts the cream
    - Viognier – floral and soft, matches the tomato-butter richness
  beer:
    - Hefeweizen – soft, low bitterness, banana and clove play with garam masala
    - Amber Lager – malt sweetness rounds off the sauce
ingredients:
  - section: Marinade
    items:
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1 inch (2.5 cm) chunks
      - 2/3 cup (150 g) plain yogurt
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) lemon juice
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) garam masala
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) ground cumin
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) paprika
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) salt
  - section: Sauce
    items:
      - 3 tbsp (45 g) butter
      - 1 onion, finely chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) grated ginger
      - 14 oz (400 g) canned tomatoes
  - section: Finish
    items:
      - 1 cup (250 ml) cream
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed
      - Salt
      - 1/4 cup (5 g) cilantro leaves
steps:
  - Mix the chicken with the yogurt, lemon juice, garam masala, cumin, paprika, and salt. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hr.
  - Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat. Cook the onion 8–10 min until golden, then the garlic and ginger 1 min. Add the tomatoes, crush them, and simmer 20–25 min until thick. Let cool a few minutes, then blend smooth.
  - While the sauce simmers, heat the broiler on high with a rack about 4 inches (10 cm) below it. Wipe excess marinade off the chicken and set it on a wire rack over a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet. Broil 10–14 min, flipping once, until charred in spots; the yogurt chars fast, so watch it.
  - Return the sauce to the pot and add the chicken and any juices. Simmer gently 6–8 min until the largest chunk reads at least 165°F (74°C).
  - Off the heat, stir in the cream and kasuri methi. Do not boil after this. Season with salt and top with cilantro.
notes: Serves 4. The original quick butter chicken, now with the chicken broiled separately for char, a longer tomato simmer, and kasuri methi at the end. The full version has a longer marinade, whole spices, and a strained sauce.
