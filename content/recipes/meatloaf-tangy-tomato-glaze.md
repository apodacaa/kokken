ingredients:
  - section: Meatloaf
    items:
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) ground beef
      - 1 onion, finely chopped
      - 1 egg
      - 1 cup (80 g) breadcrumbs
      - 1/4 cup (60 ml) milk
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) Worcestershire sauce
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) salt, 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) pepper
  - section: Glaze
    items:
      - 1/2 cup (120 ml) ketchup
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) brown sugar
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) mustard
steps:
  - Mix beef, onion, egg, breadcrumbs, milk, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper. Shape into a loaf.
  - Stir glaze and spread over loaf. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for 50–60 minutes to 160°F (71°C).
