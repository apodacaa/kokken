ingredients:
  - section: Meatloaf
    items:
      - 700 g (1 1/2 lb) ground beef
      - 1 onion, finely chopped
      - 1 egg
      - 80 g (1 cup) breadcrumbs
      - 60 ml (1/4 cup) milk
      - 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
      - 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper
  - section: Glaze
    items:
      - 120 ml (1/2 cup) ketchup
      - 1 tbsp brown sugar
      - 1 tsp mustard
steps:
  - Mix beef, onion, egg, breadcrumbs, milk, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper. Shape into a loaf.
  - Stir glaze and spread over loaf. Bake at 180°C / 350°F for 50–60 minutes to 70°C / 160°F.
