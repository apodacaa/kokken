title: Ground Chicken and Rice Soup
slug: ground-chicken-and-rice-soup
layout: layouts/recipe.njk

pairings:
  wine:
    - Unoaked Chardonnay (Chablis) – lean and lightly buttery, matching the broth without weighing it down
    - Dry Riesling – bright acid lifts the salty stock and fresh dill or parsley
  beer:
    - Kölsch – soft and clean, it leaves the delicate broth in front
    - Belgian Blonde – gentle malt and a peppery yeast edge suit the browned chicken and black pepper
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) olive oil
      - 1 lb (450 g) ground chicken
      - Salt and black pepper
      - 2 tbsp (30 g) unsalted butter
      - 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
      - 8 cups (2 L) chicken stock, plus 1–2 cups (250–500 ml) for reheating
      - 2 bay leaves or thyme sprigs, or 1 tsp (1 g) dried thyme
      - 1/4 cup (15 g) finely chopped parsley or dill, stems reserved, plus more for garnish
      - 3/4 cup (140 g) long-grain white rice, rinsed
      - 2 medium carrots, cut into 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces
      - 2 celery stalks, cut into 1/2-inch (1 cm) pieces
      - 1/2 lemon
steps:
  - Heat the olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the ground chicken, season with salt and pepper, and press it into an even layer. Cook undisturbed until well browned underneath, 4–5 minutes.
  - Break the chicken into bite-size pieces and cook, stirring, until no pink shows on the outside, 2–3 minutes more. It finishes cooking in the simmering broth; 165°F (74°C) is the safe temperature.
  - Lower the heat to medium and add the butter and onion. Season with salt and pepper and cook, scraping up the browned bits, until the onion is softened but not browned, 5–7 minutes.
  - Add the stock, bay leaves or thyme, and the parsley or dill stems and bring to a boil over high heat.
  - Add the rice, carrots and celery. Lower to a steady simmer and set the lid ajar so the stock doesn't reduce too far. Cook, stirring occasionally so the rice doesn't stick, until the rice is tender, 15–18 minutes.
  - Pluck out the bay leaves or thyme sprigs and the herb stems, stir in the chopped parsley or dill, and season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon juice. Serve garnished with more herbs.
notes: Serves 4–6. Browning the chicken in one flat layer before breaking it up gives the broth a browned, savory depth and leaves chunkier, more tender pieces than crumbling it raw. Ground chicken is lean, so the oil stops it sticking; ground thigh stays juicier than breast. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear so the broth stays clearer and less gummy. Rice swells to about three times its size and keeps drinking broth as the soup sits, so leftovers thicken into something like a stew; loosen them with the extra stock when reheating. If you plan on leftovers, cook the rice separately and spoon it into each bowl. Refrigerate soup and rice within 2 hours, eat within 3–4 days, and reheat to a boil. Brown rice works too but needs 35–40 minutes, so add the carrots and celery halfway through. A variation of Chicken and Rice Soup.
