  beer:
    - Amber Lager – malt depth
    - Saison – herbal, dry
ingredients:
  - section: Components
    items:
      - 2 cups cooked grains (quinoa, farro, or rice)
      - Assorted vegetables (sweet potato, broccoli, chickpeas)
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
      - Salt and pepper
  - section: Tahini Dressing
    items:
      - 3 tbsp (45 ml) tahini
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) lemon juice
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) maple syrup
      - Water to loosen
      - Salt
steps:
  - Roast veg at 425°F (220°C) tossed with oil, salt, and pepper until browned.
  - Whisk dressing ingredients smooth.
  - Serve bowls with grains, roasted veg, and drizzle of tahini dressing.
