ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 tbsp oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 700 g (1 1/2 lb) ground beef
      - 2 tbsp chili powder
      - 1 tsp cumin
      - 1 tsp smoked paprika
      - 400 g (14 oz) tomatoes
      - 400 g (14 oz) kidney beans, drained
      - 250 ml (1 cup) beef stock
      - Salt, sugar to balance, lime
steps:
  - Sauté onion and garlic; add beef and brown.
  - Stir in spices; cook 1 minute. Add tomatoes, beans, and stock; simmer 30–40 minutes.
  - Season to taste; finish with a squeeze of lime.
