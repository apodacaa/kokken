---
title: Classic Beef Chili
slug: classic-beef-chili
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Zinfandel – jammy fruit softens chili heat
    - Syrah – smoke and pepper echo the cumin and paprika
  beer:
    - Amber Lager – malty and easy; hop bitterness would amplify the heat
    - Brown Ale – nutty roast for the beef and beans
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 1/2 lb (700 g) ground beef
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) chili powder
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) cumin
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) smoked paprika
      - 14 oz (400 g) tomatoes
      - 14 oz (400 g) kidney beans, drained
      - 1 cup (250 ml) beef stock
      - Salt, sugar to balance, lime
steps:
  - Sauté onion and garlic; add beef and brown.
  - Stir in spices; cook 1 minute. Add tomatoes, beans, and stock; simmer 30–40 minutes.
  - Season to taste; finish with a squeeze of lime.
