---
title: Roasted Cauliflower with Tahini Sauce
slug: roasted-cauliflower-with-tahini-sauce
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Grüner Veltliner – peppery and herbal with tahini
    - Chenin Blanc (dry) – waxy texture for roasted cauliflower
  beer:
    - Pale Lager – crisp against nutty tahini
    - Saison – herbal and dry
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 cauliflower, florets
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
      - Salt and pepper
      - Sauce: 3 tbsp (45 ml) tahini, 1 tbsp (15 ml) lemon juice, 1 small garlic clove grated, water to loosen, salt
steps:
  - Toss cauliflower with oil, salt, and pepper; roast at 425°F (220°C) for 20–25 minutes until browned.
  - Whisk tahini, lemon, garlic, and water to drizzle; season. Serve over cauliflower; add herbs if desired.
