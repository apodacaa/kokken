title: Braised Short Ribs
slug: braised-short-ribs
tags:
  - main
  - beef
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1.5 kg (3 lb) beef short ribs
      - 2 tbsp oil
      - 2 onions, chopped
      - 2 carrots, chopped
      - 3 cloves garlic, minced
      - 2 tbsp tomato paste
      - 2 cups (500 ml) red wine
      - 2 cups (500 ml) beef stock
      - 2 sprigs rosemary, 2 sprigs thyme
      - Salt and pepper
steps:
  - Season ribs; sear in oil until browned; remove.
  - Sauté onions, carrots, and garlic; add tomato paste; cook 1 minute.
  - Deglaze with wine; add stock and herbs. Return ribs; cover and braise at 160°C / 325°F for 3 hours until tender.
