---
title: Mexican Crema (overnight)
slug: mexican-crema
tags:
  - sauce
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items: 
      - 1 cup (240 ml) cream
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) buttermilk
      - 1/8 tsp (0.5 ml) salt
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) lime juice
steps:
  - Stir cream and buttermilk. Store at room temp for 12 hours.
  - Mix in salt and lime juice (can be stored for up to 2 months).
