---
title: Veggie Sushi Rolls
slug: veggie-sushi-rolls
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Dry Riesling – clean acidity for soy and wasabi
    - Champagne (Brut) – dry bubbles for rice and nori
  beer:
    - Japanese Lager – dry, clean
    - Rice Lager – light and neutral
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 3 nori sheets
      - 2 cups (400 g) sushi rice, cooked & seasoned
      - Cucumber, carrot, avocado, bell pepper (matchsticks)
      - Soy sauce, wasabi, pickled ginger
steps:
  - Spread rice over nori; arrange vegetables.
  - Roll tightly and slice. Serve with soy, wasabi, and ginger.
---
