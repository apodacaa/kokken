ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 200 g (1 cup) red lentils, rinsed
      - 1 L (4 cups) water or stock
      - 2 tbsp ghee or oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tbsp grated ginger
      - 1 tsp cumin seeds
      - 1/2 tsp turmeric
      - Salt, cilantro
steps:
  - Simmer lentils in water until tender, 15–20 minutes.
  - Heat ghee; sizzle cumin seeds; add onion, garlic, and ginger; cook until golden.
  - Stir turmeric into tempering; pour into lentils; season and garnish.
