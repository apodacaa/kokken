---
title: Lentil Dal with Ginger & Garlic
slug: lentil-dal-ginger-garlic
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Dry Riesling – acid and lime-like freshness for earthy lentils
    - Chenin Blanc (dry) – waxy, fruity, handles ginger
  beer:
    - Pilsner – clean and crisp
    - Witbier – soft citrus and spice
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 cup (200 g) red lentils, rinsed
      - 4 cups (1 L) water or stock
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) ghee or oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) grated ginger
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) cumin seeds
      - 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml) turmeric
      - Salt, cilantro
steps:
  - Simmer lentils in water until tender, 15–20 minutes.
  - Heat ghee; sizzle cumin seeds; add onion, garlic, and ginger; cook until golden.
  - Stir turmeric into tempering; pour into lentils; season and garnish.
