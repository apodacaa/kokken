ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 500 g (1 lb) ground pork
      - 4 cloves garlic, minced
      - 2–3 red Thai chilies, sliced
      - 1 tbsp vegetable oil
      - 1 tbsp soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp fish sauce
      - 2 tsp sugar
      - 1 tbsp oyster sauce
      - 1 bunch Thai basil leaves
      - (Optional) 2 fried eggs, steamed rice
steps:
  - Heat oil; sauté garlic and chilies until fragrant.
  - Add pork; cook until browned and crumbly.
  - Stir in soy, fish sauce, sugar, and oyster sauce.
  - Toss in basil until just wilted. Serve over rice; top with fried egg.
