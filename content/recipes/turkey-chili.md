---
title: Turkey Chili
slug: turkey-chili
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Zinfandel – jammy fruit and pepper stand up to chipotle heat
    - Malbec – plush dark fruit softens the smoke and spice
  beer:
    - Amber Lager – malty sweetness tames the heat
    - Porter – roasty notes echo the cocoa and chipotle
notes: Serves 4–6. Garnish with sour cream, scallions, cilantro and lime wedges.
ingredients:
  - section: Turkey
    items:
      - 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
      - 2.2 lb (1 kg) ground turkey
      - 3/4 teaspoon salt, divided, plus 1/2 teaspoon for the vegetables
      - 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
      - 3 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tablespoon tomato paste
      - 1 tablespoon chipotle powder
      - 1 tablespoon chile powder
      - 1/2 teaspoon coriander
      - 1 pinch cinnamon
      - 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  - section: Vegetables
    items:
      - 1 large onion, chopped
      - 1 green bell pepper, chopped
      - 1 jalapeño, chopped
  - section: Simmer
    items:
      - 1 14-oz (400 g) can tomato purée
      - 1 15-oz (425 g) can kidney beans, drained
      - 6 oz (180 ml) beer, about 1/2 bottle
      - 1 teaspoon oregano
      - 1 1/2 cups (360 ml) chicken stock
  - section: To serve
    items:
      - Sour cream
      - Scallions, sliced
      - Cilantro
      - Lime wedges
steps:
  - In a large pot over medium-high heat, warm 1 tablespoon of the oil. Brown the turkey with 3/4 teaspoon salt and the pepper, stirring occasionally, until golden, 6–8 minutes.
  - Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, chipotle powder, chile powder, coriander, cinnamon and cocoa and sauté for 30 seconds. Transfer the turkey to a bowl with a slotted spoon.
  - Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil to the pot and sauté the onion, bell pepper and jalapeño with the remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt, stirring, until the onion is translucent, about 10 minutes.
  - Return the turkey and any liquid in the bowl to the pot. Add the tomato purée, beans, beer, oregano and chicken stock.
  - Simmer, partly covered, until thick enough for your taste, about 1 hour. Serve hot with the garnishes.
