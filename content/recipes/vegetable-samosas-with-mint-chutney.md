pairings:
  wine:
    - Sparkling Rosé – refreshing

  beer:
    - Pale Ale – citrus hops
    - Amber Lager – malt sweetness
ingredients:
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) cumin seeds
      - 2 potatoes, diced & boiled
      - 1 cup (150 g) peas
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) garam masala
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) ground coriander
      - Salt
  - section: Assembly
    items:
      - Pastry sheets or wrappers
  - section: Mint Chutney
    items:
      - 1 cup mint
      - 1/2 cup cilantro
      - 1 green chili
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) lemon juice
      - Pinch salt
steps:
  - Cook onion, garlic, and cumin; add potatoes, peas, and spices; season; cool.
  - Fill pastry and seal triangles; fry at 350°F (175°C) until golden or bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20–25 minutes.
  - Blend chutney ingredients smooth; serve with samosas.
