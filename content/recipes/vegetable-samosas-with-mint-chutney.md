title: Vegetable Samosas with Mint Chutney
slug: vegetable-samosas-with-mint-chutney
tags:
  - main
  - vegetarian
  - snack
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 2 tbsp oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tsp cumin seeds
      - 2 potatoes, diced & boiled
      - 1 cup (150 g) peas
      - 1 tsp garam masala
      - 1 tsp ground coriander
      - Salt
  - section: Assembly
    items:
      - Pastry sheets or wrappers
  - section: Mint Chutney
    items:
      - 1 cup mint
      - 1/2 cup cilantro
      - 1 green chili
      - 2 tbsp lemon juice
      - Pinch salt
steps:
  - Cook onion, garlic, and cumin; add potatoes, peas, and spices; season; cool.
  - Fill pastry and seal triangles; fry at 175°C / 350°F until golden or bake at 200°C / 400°F for 20–25 minutes.
  - Blend chutney ingredients smooth; serve with samosas.
