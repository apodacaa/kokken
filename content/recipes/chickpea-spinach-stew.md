---
title: Chickpea & Spinach Stew
slug: chickpea-spinach-stew
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Garnacha – juicy, low tannin, plays well with smoked paprika
    - Dry Sherry (Fino) – nutty and saline, a natural fit with chickpeas and spinach
  beer:
    - Amber Lager – malt for the chickpeas and paprika
    - Saison – dry and earthy against the greens
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) olive oil
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 14 oz (400 g) canned tomatoes
      - 2 cans (800 g / 28 oz) chickpeas, drained
      - 5 oz (150 g) spinach
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) smoked paprika
      - Salt, pepper, lemon
steps:
  - Sauté onion and garlic; add paprika.
  - Add tomatoes and chickpeas; simmer 10–15 minutes.
  - Stir in spinach to wilt; season and finish with lemon.
---
