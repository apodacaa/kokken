title: Prik Nam Pla
slug: prik-nam-pla
tags:
  - sauce
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 3-4 Thai bird's eye chilies, thinly sliced (red and/or green)
      - 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped (optional)
      - 3 tbsp fish sauce
      - 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
      - 1 tsp sugar (palm sugar preferred)
      - Optional: a few slices of shallot or a splash of water to mellow
steps:
  - Thinly slice chilies (wear gloves if sensitive). Finely chop garlic if using.
  - Combine chilies, garlic, fish sauce, lime juice, and sugar in a small bowl or jar. Stir until sugar dissolves.
  - Taste and adjust.
notes: Stores covered in the fridge 3-5 days; flavor mellows as chilies infuse.
