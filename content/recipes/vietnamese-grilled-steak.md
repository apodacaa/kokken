---
title: Vietnamese Grilled Steak
slug: vietnamese-grilled-steak
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Malbec – smoky and rich for char
    - Syrah – peppery with the chili and lime
  beer:
    - Amber Lager – caramelized malt echoes the sugar marinade
    - Dark Lager – roasted malt without bitterness
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1/2 cup (100 g) palm sugar or brown sugar
      - 1/4 cup (60 ml) water
      - 3 tablespoons (45 ml) fish sauce
      - 1/3 cup (80 ml) lime juice
      - 2 cloves garlic, grated on a microplane zester
      - 1 tablespoon Thai chile powder (or red chile flakes)
      - 1/4 cup (60 ml) vegetable oil
      - 1 whole flank steak, about 2 lb (900 g)
steps:
  - Combine water and sugar in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring until dissolved. Transfer to a small bowl; add fish sauce, lime juice, garlic, and chile powder and stir to combine. Reserve half of this mixture for later use.
  - Whisk oil into the remaining half. Add flank steak to a gallon-sized zipper-lock bag with this marinade, press out air, seal, and marinate—turning occasionally—for at least 1 hour and up to 12 hours.
  - Grill until well done.
