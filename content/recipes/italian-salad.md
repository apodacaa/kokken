---
title: Italian Salad
slug: italian-salad
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Verdicchio – almond and herbal; stands up to the vinegar dressing
    - Vermentino – crisp and savory for peppers and provolone
  beer:
    - Pilsner – crisp against the vinegar and salt
    - Italian-style Pale Lager – light, bitter finish for pepperoncini
ingredients:
  - section: Salad
    items:
      - 1 head of iceberg lettuce, washed and torn
      - Red onions, thinly sliced, salted and pressed
      - Provolone and/or mozz balls
      - Roasted red peppers and/or pepperoncini
  - section: Dressing
    items:
      - 1/2 cup (120 ml) olive oil
      - 5 tbsp (75 ml) white wine vinegar
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) mayo
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) sugar
      - 1 1/2 tsp (7.5 ml) oregano
      - 1 garlic clove, grated
      - Salt and pepper to taste
steps:
  - Mix dressing.
  - Toss salad in dressing.
