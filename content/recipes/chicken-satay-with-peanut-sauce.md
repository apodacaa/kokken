---
title: Chicken Satay with Peanut Sauce
slug: chicken-satay-with-peanut-sauce
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Off-dry Riesling – acidity and a touch of sweetness for peanut and char
    - Pinot Gris (Alsace) – round body for the rich sauce
  beer:
    - Lager – clean refresher against the sweet-salty peanut sauce
    - Amber Ale – caramel malt echoes the grilled edges
ingredients:
  - section: Satay
    items:
      - 1 1/3 lb (600 g) chicken breast or thigh, strips
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) fish sauce
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) brown sugar
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) curry powder
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil
  - section: Peanut Sauce
    items:
      - 3 tbsp (45 ml) peanut butter
      - 2/3 cup (150 ml) coconut milk
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) soy sauce
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) sugar
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) lime juice
      - Chili to taste
steps:
  - Marinate chicken with soy, fish sauce, sugar, curry powder, and oil (30 minutes). Thread onto skewers.
  - Grill or broil 2–3 minutes per side until cooked.
  - Simmer peanut sauce ingredients to combine; serve with skewers.
