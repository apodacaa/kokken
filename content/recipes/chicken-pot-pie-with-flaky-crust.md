---
title: Chicken Pot Pie with Flaky Crust
slug: chicken-pot-pie-with-flaky-crust
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Chardonnay (unoaked) – bright acidity
    - Pinot Noir – light red with earthy tones
  beer:
    - Golden Ale – malty backbone
    - Brown Ale – nutty, warming
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) butter
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 carrots, diced
      - 2 celery stalks, diced
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) flour
      - 2 cups (500 ml) chicken stock
      - 1 lb (500 g) cooked chicken, cubed
      - 1 cup (150 g) peas
      - 1 sheet puff pastry
      - Salt and pepper
steps:
  - Sauté onion, carrot, and celery in butter; stir in flour 1 minute.
  - Whisk in stock; simmer to thicken. Add chicken and peas; season.
  - Transfer to dish; top with pastry; cut vents. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 25–30 minutes until golden.
