---
title: Ratatouille
slug: ratatouille
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Provence Rosé – herbal, dry and made for this
    - Côtes du Rhône – peppery fruit for the roasted vegetables
  beer:
    - Saison – rustic and herbal
    - Amber Lager – gentle malt with tomato and eggplant
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 eggplant, diced
      - 2 zucchinis, sliced
      - 1 red pepper, chopped
      - 1 onion, chopped
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 14 oz (400 g) tomatoes (canned or fresh)
steps:
  - Sauté each vegetable separately in a little oil until lightly browned; set aside.
  - Combine vegetables with garlic and tomatoes; simmer 30 minutes. Season to taste.
