---
title: Deviled Eggs
slug: deviled-eggs
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Brut Champagne or Crémant – bubbles and acidity cut the rich yolk filling
    - Dry Riesling – crisp and slightly sweet, lifts the mustard and Tabasco
  beer:
    - Pilsner – clean and crisp, refreshes between bites
    - Saison – peppery and dry, echoes the pepper and chives
ingredients:
  - section: Eggs
    items:
      - 6 large eggs
  - section: Filling
    items:
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) Dijon mustard
      - 1 to 2 dashes Tabasco sauce, to taste
      - Salt, to taste
      - 1/4 tsp (1 ml) freshly ground black pepper
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) snipped fresh chives
      - 3 tbsp (45 ml) mayonnaise
  - section: Garnish
    items:
      - Paprika
      - Whole fresh chives
steps:
  - Rinse the eggs with warm water and place in a small saucepan. Cover with cold water, set over medium-high heat, and bring to a boil. Turn off the heat, cover, and let sit 10–12 min. Drain, then rinse under cold water or move to a bowl of ice water, and peel. Cool in the refrigerator, loosely covered, 15 min.
  - Halve the eggs lengthwise and carefully remove the yolks. Mash the yolks in a bowl with a fork. Stir in the mustard, Tabasco, salt, pepper, snipped chives, and mayonnaise.
  - Fill each egg white with about 1 1/2 tsp (7 ml) of the yolk mixture, using a spoon or a piping bag fitted with a star tip. Dust with paprika, arrange on a platter, and garnish with whole chives.
notes: Makes 12 halves.
