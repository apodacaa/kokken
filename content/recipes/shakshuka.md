---
title: Shakshuka (Eggs in Spiced Tomato Sauce)
slug: shakshuka
tags:
  - main
  - vegetarian
  - eggs
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 2 tbsp olive oil
      - 1 onion, sliced
      - 1 red bell pepper, sliced
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 1 tsp cumin
      - 1 tsp paprika
      - 14 oz (400 g) canned tomatoes
      - 4–6 eggs
      - Salt, parsley
steps:
  - Heat oil; sauté onion, pepper, and garlic until soft. Add cumin and paprika; stir.
  - Add tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes. Make wells; crack in eggs.
  - Cover and cook until eggs set to your liking. Season; garnish with parsley.
