ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 400 g (14 oz) elbow macaroni
      - 3 tbsp butter
      - 3 tbsp flour
      - 750 ml (3 cups) milk
      - 200 g (2 cups) cheddar, grated
      - 100 g (1 cup) Gruyère, grated
      - Salt, pepper, pinch nutmeg
steps:
  - Cook pasta until al dente; drain.
  - Melt butter; whisk in flour 1 minute; gradually whisk in milk until smooth.
  - Stir in cheeses to melt; season. Combine with pasta. Bake at 190°C / 375°F for 15–20 minutes if desired.
