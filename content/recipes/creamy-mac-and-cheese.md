---
title: Creamy Mac & Cheese
slug: creamy-mac-and-cheese
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Chardonnay (lightly oaked) – butter and weight mirror the cheese
    - Chenin Blanc – acidity cuts the richness
  beer:
    - Helles – clean and malty, refreshes the palate
    - Belgian Tripel – spicy fruit and high carbonation scrub the richness
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 14 oz (400 g) elbow macaroni
      - 3 tbsp (45 ml) butter
      - 3 tbsp (45 ml) flour
      - 3 cups (750 ml) milk
      - 2 cups (200 g) cheddar, grated
      - 1 cup (100 g) Gruyère, grated
      - Salt, pepper, pinch nutmeg
steps:
  - Cook pasta until al dente; drain.
  - Melt butter; whisk in flour 1 minute; gradually whisk in milk until smooth.
  - Stir in cheeses to melt; season. Combine with pasta. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 15–20 minutes if desired.
