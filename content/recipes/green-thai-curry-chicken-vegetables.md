---
title: Green Thai Curry with Chicken & Vegetables
slug: green-thai-curry-chicken-vegetables
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Off-dry Riesling – the standard answer to coconut and heat
    - Grüner Veltliner – peppery and herbal like the Thai basil
  beer:
    - Witbier – coriander and citrus, gentle bitterness
    - Lager – crisp and cold; avoids hop-amplified heat
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) oil
      - 2–3 tbsp (30–45 ml) green curry paste
      - 14 oz (400 ml) coconut milk
      - 1 lb (500 g) chicken thigh, sliced
      - 1 cup mixed vegetables (bell pepper, zucchini, green beans)
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) fish sauce
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) sugar
      - Basil leaves, lime wedges
steps:
  - Fry curry paste in oil 1 minute; add coconut milk; bring to simmer.
  - Add chicken; cook 8–10 minutes. Add vegetables; simmer until tender.
  - Season with fish sauce and sugar. Add basil and serve with lime.
