ingredients:
  - "1 onion"
  - "2 carrots"
  - "2 celery stalks"
  - "1 lb ground beef"
  - "1/2 cup dry red wine"
  - "2 cups beef broth"
  - "12 oz canned tomatoes"
steps:
  - "Saute onions, carrots, celery."
  - "Add beef until cooked."
  - "Add wine, broth, tomatoes."
  - "Simmer until thick."
notes: "Healthy pinches of salt as you go."
