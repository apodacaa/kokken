title: Carne Asada
slug: carne-asada
tags:
  - beef
  - main
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items: 
      - 1 (2 lb) skirt/flank steak, trimmed, pounded 1/4-inch thick and cut with grain into 4 equal steaks
      - 2 tsp salt
      - 3/4 tsp ground cumin
steps:
  - Pat steak dry.
  - Sprinkle salt and cumin.
  - Leave uncovered in fridge on wire rack for 24 hours
  - Grill 3-4 minutes on each side until medium done (135)
  - Tent and let rest for 5 minutes
