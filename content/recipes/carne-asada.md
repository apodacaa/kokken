---
title: Carne Asada
slug: carne-asada
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Zinfandel – ripe fruit and spice for the char and cumin
    - Tempranillo (Crianza) – savory, medium tannin for flank steak
  beer:
    - Mexican Lager with lime – classic, cold relief against the grill
    - Amber Lager – caramelized malt echoes the sear
ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items: 
      - 1 (2 lb/900 g) skirt/flank steak, trimmed, pounded 1/4-inch thick and cut with grain into 4 equal steaks
      - 2 tsp (10 ml) salt
      - 3/4 tsp (4 ml) ground cumin
steps:
  - Pat steak dry.
  - Sprinkle salt and cumin.
  - Leave uncovered in fridge on wire rack for 24 hours.
  - Grill 3–4 minutes on each side until medium, 135°F (57°C).
  - Tent and let rest for 5 minutes.
