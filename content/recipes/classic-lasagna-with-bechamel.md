ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 12 lasagna noodles
      - 500 g (1 lb) ground beef
      - 1 onion, diced
      - 700 ml (24 oz) marinara
      - 3 tbsp butter
      - 3 tbsp flour
      - 750 ml (3 cups) milk
      - 200 g (2 cups) mozzarella, shredded
steps:
  - Cook beef with onion; stir in marinara and simmer briefly.
  - Make béchamel: melt butter, whisk in flour, then gradually whisk in milk until smooth and thickened.
  - Layer noodles, meat sauce, béchamel, and mozzarella; repeat layers.
  - Bake at 190°C / 375°F for 40 minutes until bubbly and golden.
