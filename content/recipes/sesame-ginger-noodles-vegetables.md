ingredients:
  - section: _main_
    items:
      - 10 oz (300 g) noodles (udon/lo mein/spaghetti)
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) sesame oil
      - 2 tbsp (30 ml) soy sauce
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) rice vinegar
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) honey or sugar
      - 1 tbsp (15 ml) grated ginger
      - 2 cloves garlic, minced
      - 2 cups mixed veg (carrot, pepper, broccoli)
      - Sesame seeds, scallions
steps:
  - Cook noodles; rinse briefly.
  - Stir-fry veg in a little oil until crisp-tender. Whisk sesame oil, soy, vinegar, honey, ginger, and garlic.
  - Toss noodles with veg and sauce; garnish with sesame seeds and scallions.
