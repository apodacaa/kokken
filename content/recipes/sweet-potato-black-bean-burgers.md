---
title: Sweet Potato & Black Bean Burgers
slug: sweet-potato-black-bean-burgers
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Garnacha – fruity & earthy
    - Zinfandel – bold & juicy
  beer:
    - Amber Ale – malt sweetness
    - Brown Ale – nutty depth
ingredients:
  - section: Patties
    items:
      - 14 oz (400 g) sweet potato, roasted & mashed
      - 1 can (400 g / 14 oz) black beans, rinsed
      - 1 cup (80 g) breadcrumbs or oats
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) cumin
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) chili powder
      - Salt and pepper
  - section: To Serve
    items:
      - Buns and fixings
steps:
  - Mash sweet potato and beans; mix with breadcrumbs and spices; season.
  - Form patties; pan-sear in a little oil 3–4 minutes per side or bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20 minutes.
  - Serve on buns with your favorite toppings.
