ingredients:
  - section: Patties
    items:
      - 400 g (14 oz) sweet potato, roasted & mashed
      - 1 can (400 g / 14 oz) black beans, rinsed
      - 80 g (1 cup) breadcrumbs or oats
      - 1 tsp cumin
      - 1 tsp chili powder
      - Salt and pepper
  - section: To Serve
    items:
      - Buns and fixings
steps:
  - Mash sweet potato and beans; mix with breadcrumbs and spices; season.
  - Form patties; pan-sear in a little oil 3–4 minutes per side or bake at 200°C / 400°F for 20 minutes.
  - Serve on buns with your favorite toppings.
