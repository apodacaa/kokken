---
title: Sheet-Pan Feta with Broccolini and Tomatoes
slug: sheet-pan-feta-broccolini
layout: layouts/recipe.njk
permalink: /recipes/{{ slug }}/
pairings:
  wine:
    - Assyrtiko – mineral and lemony, made for feta and roasted tomato
    - Dry Rosé – bright and crisp, handles the cumin and chile heat
  beer:
    - Witbier – citrus and coriander echo the lemon and cilantro
    - Pilsner – clean and crisp against the salty, creamy feta
ingredients:
  - section: Sheet pan
    items:
      - 1 bunch broccolini, ends trimmed, thick stalks split lengthwise, or broccoli, stalks trimmed and cut into bite-size pieces
      - 1 pint (475 ml) grape tomatoes, halved (about 2 cups)
      - 1 small red onion, peeled, quartered, and cut into 2 inch (5 cm) wedges
      - 1 lemon, 1/2 cut into thin rounds and the remaining 1/2 left intact, for serving
      - 3 tbsp (45 ml) olive oil, plus more for serving
      - 1 tsp (5 ml) ground cumin
      - 1/2 tsp (2 ml) red-pepper flakes
      - Kosher salt and black pepper
      - 2 blocks (6–8 oz / 170–225 g each) feta, cut into 1 inch (2.5 cm) slices
  - section: To serve
    items:
      - Cooked orzo or farro
      - 1/2 cup (10 g) fresh basil or cilantro leaves and fine stems, roughly chopped (optional)
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). On a large rimmed baking sheet, toss the broccolini, tomatoes, onion, and lemon rounds with the olive oil, cumin, red-pepper flakes, and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Go light on the salt, since the feta is salty.
  - Nestle the feta slices among the vegetables and drizzle them with a little more oil.
  - Roast 20–25 min, until the broccolini is charred at the edges, the tomatoes have burst, and the feta is golden in spots.
  - Spoon over orzo or farro. Squeeze the remaining lemon half over the top, drizzle with olive oil, and scatter with the herbs.
notes: Serves 4. Broccoli works in place of broccolini; cut the stalks into bite-size pieces so they roast through in the same time.
